## Before you start

**Upload these reference images with every prompt** so the characters stay the same in every scene:
1. The i-Nova image (`art/inova_src.jpg`: the hooded figure of light on the glowing platform).
2. The "Meet the Team" image (Newton, Byte and SparX).
3. For Images 3 and 5 only: the old activity sheet it replaces (`ch2/media/image4.png` and `ch2/media/image6.png`), so the new sheet keeps the same look.

**Settings:** square, **2048 × 2048 px or larger**. Generate at the highest quality your tool offers.

**Keep words out of the story pictures (Images 1, 2 and 4).** Image tools often misspell them. Every label the story needs, like "LARGE BEE — 41% SURE", is already in the page layout. The two activity sheets (Images 3 and 5) need words. Check every word carefully after generating, and regenerate if any are misspelled.

**To drop a finished picture in:** in Word, right-click the placeholder › **Change Picture** › **From a File**. It keeps the exact size and position. Or send the files back to me and I'll place them and rebalance the pages.
